🧾 Billy Privacy Policy

Effective 12 November 2026

Billy is an app for splitting bills with friends. This policy explains what Billy collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it. Billy never moves money and never sees your bank or card details.

What Billy collects

DataWhy
Name, email address, phone number (optional), profile photo (from Google sign-in)Your account, and so friends can recognise you on shared bills.
UPI IDSo people on your bills can pay you with their UPI app. It is shown to people who share a bill with you.
Bills: items, prices, taxes, who shared what, payment status, UPI transaction referenceTo split bills and track who has paid.
Saved groups (names and phone numbers you choose)To reuse the same people on new bills.
Receipt photosTo read the bill. Photos stay on your phone; a compressed copy is sent for reading (see below) and is not stored by Billy.
Contacts (only if you allow it)To pick people for a bill. Billy reads your contact list on the phone and saves only the people you choose.
Bill files you upload (PDF, image, email, text, Word or Excel)To read the bill. The file is sent for reading (see below) and is not stored by Billy; only the bill you save is kept.

Billy does not collect your location, show ads or sell data.

Who it is shared with

  • People on the same bill see the bill, your name, your share, payment status and your UPI ID.
  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore, stored in Mumbai, India) keeps your account, profile, bills and saved groups so they sync between phones.
  • Google Gemini API reads receipt photos, the text read from them on your phone, and bill files you upload (a PDF as it is; for other files, the text read from them on your phone), and returns the items and totals. Billy uses Gemini’s free tier, under which Google may use what is sent to improve its products. Avoid scanning receipts that show sensitive personal details.
  • Your UPI app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm…) opens with the amount and the receiver’s UPI ID when you tap Pay. The payment happens entirely in that app.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Profile → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account, profile, UPI ID, saved groups, and the bills you created (for everyone on them), and clears Billy’s data from your phone. Bills that friends created stay with them, with your name as it appears on their bill.

You can also turn off Cloud sync in Profile to keep new bills on your phone only, or email us to ask for deletion.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. Firestore security rules allow a bill to be read only by the people on it, and only its creator can change or delete it.

Children

Billy is not meant for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
